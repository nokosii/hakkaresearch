研究專長：

development research, state research, agrarian change research, food sovereignty and dietary culture
個人簡介：
我的博士論文〈「永續」？「發展」？農法變革中的論述與實作：以玉里稻作為主的分析〉（王乃雯 2023）主要關注於臺灣近三十年稻作農法變革的脈絡，與其在地方上所形塑的新興社會網絡。另一方面，本論文同時指出當國家系統在全球自由化貿易壓力下，亟欲將普惠式的公糧政策逐步取消之際，新興節點國家政策所引領的「粗放化」與「精緻化」，除因路線矛盾造成農地競租，也由於市場化的趨勢，加快稻作農村走向分化的速度。
在國科會人社中心擔任博士級研究人員期間，我將研究重心擴展至農地碳權作為新興交換價值/權利，對三農（農民、農村、農業）議題的可能影響。
未來，結合過往在兩岸農村長期的田野調查經歷，我期待能以「觀光」作為媒介，探討其作為一種公共人類學形式的可能性。
My doctoral dissertation examines the context of changes in rice-farming practices in Taiwan over the past three decades, and the new social networks that have emerged locally through these transformations. It also argues that, as the state system has sought to gradually roll back universal public grain policies under the pressures of global trade liberalization, the “extensification” and “intensification” driven by an emerging node-state policy agenda have not only generated competition for leasing farmland due to internal policy contradictions, but have also accelerated social differentiation in rice-growing rural communities as marketization deepens. I am currently focusing on farmland carbon credits as an emergent form of exchange value and rights, and their potential implications for the “three rural issues” (farmers, rural communities, and agriculture). Looking ahead, I hope to use tourism as a medium to explore its possibilities as a form of public anthropology.
